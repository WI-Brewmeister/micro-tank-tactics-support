MICRO-TANK TACTICS

EVERY HIT MUST BANK

Micro-Tank Tactics is a neon, turn-based tank duel for iPhone. Bounce your shots off walls and blocks to hit the enemy tank. Play against the computer, or pass one phone between two players.

Contact support

Questions, bug reports or purchase problems? Email support@getkeymiles.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if something looks wrong.

How to play

  • Drag left and right in the box under the arena to aim. Slow drags give fine control.
  • Tap the box to fire.
  • Hits only count after your shot bounces off a wall or block at least once. Straight shots fizzle.
  • A tank that gets hit drives to a new spot, so find a new angle.
  • REDEPLOY lets you skip a shot to move your own tank (once per level).
  • Replay the tutorial any time with HOW TO PLAY on the title screen.

Purchases

The first 5 games are free. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks unlimited play. If you reinstall the game or move to a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases on the title screen to get your unlock back at no charge. Refunds are handled by Apple through reportaproblem.apple.com.

No sound?

The game follows your iPhone's Silent mode. Turn Silent mode off to hear sound. The speaker button in the bottom-right corner turns the music on or off.
