PRIVACY POLICY

Micro-Tank Tactics · Effective November 17, 2026

Micro-Tank Tactics ("the game") is made by Terry Taylor. This policy explains what information the game handles. In short: the game does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts and no sign-in. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Information stored on your device

The game saves a few settings on your iPhone so it remembers them between sessions: whether music is muted, whether you have seen the tutorial, how many free games you have played, and whether you have unlocked the full game. This information stays on your device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the game deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. We never see or receive your name, Apple ID, payment card or billing details. Apple's handling of that information is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Beta testing and crash reports

If you test pre-release versions through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with us, as described in Apple's TestFlight terms. If you have enabled sharing with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may also share anonymous crash and usage data with us. We use this only to fix problems in the game.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Contacting us

If you email us for support, we use your email address and message only to reply to you, and we don't share them. Questions about this policy: support@getkeymiles.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
